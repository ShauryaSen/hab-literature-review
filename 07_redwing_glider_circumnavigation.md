# Redwing Glider Global Circumnavigation (Sentinel Mission)

**Authors:** Rutgers University & Teledyne Marine collaboration  
**Year:** 2023 (announced) / 2025 (launched)  
**Venue:** Rutgers Marine Science  
https://www.teledynemarine.com/sentinelmission

This is essentially the marine version of the balloon navigation. I think the methods that we develop for loons would transfer very well to the AUV scenario exploiting ocean currents, and this project is a proof of concept of this idea.

---
## Motivation
- like with the stratosphere, there is data scarcity in the ocean as many of the regions are unsampled, redwing is meant to fill these gaps for long term climate prediction.

## Core Methodology
**Long-endurance buoyancy-driven autonomous navigation** exploiting major ocean current systems for global circumnavigation.
- The glider changes its density relative to seawater by shifting oil between internal and external bladders. 
-  GPS at the surface; dead-reckoning underwater.
- Expected 5-year mission duration. The glider consumes energy only for buoyancy changes, heading control, and sensor/communication payloads.
