# NASA STRATO: Wildfire Monitoring & Communications via Stratospheric Balloons

**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** NASA Program (Armstrong Flight Research Center / Aerostar collaboration)  
**Link:** https://www.nasa.gov/stmd-flight-opportunities/flight-summaries/improving-firefighter-safety-with-strato/

This is a compelling application of balloon navigation and precise station-keeping. NASA deployed a balloon with LTE communication and IR imaging over idaho wildfires for 11 days to provide cellular coverage and live thermal data to firefighters.

## Motivation
Communication with wildland fire fighters in the field was difficult due to rugged terrain and limited cell phone coverage in remote areas. 

STRATO provides persistent cell coverage from the stratosphere, enabling real-time communication between firefighters and command posts. It also uses an infrared sensor to provide valuable heat and spatial information to help them better understand the fire’s characteristics.
